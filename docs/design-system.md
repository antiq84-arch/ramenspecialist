# RamenSpecialist.com — design system

## Direction

Warm, clear and trustworthy for Belgian homeowners aged roughly 30–60. The site must feel premium but approachable: light surfaces, architectural photography without people, anthracite as contrast and orange only for decisions and calls to action.

## Tokens

| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Warm paper | `#FAF8F4` |
| Warm grey | `#F1EEE8` |
| Anthracite | `#292D2F` |
| Graphite | `#34393B` |
| CTA orange | `#D85B22` |
| CTA orange hover | `#B94718` |
| Status green | `#35B86B` |
| Border | `#DEDAD2` |

- Display: DM Serif Display.
- UI/body: DM Sans.
- Radius: 9 px controls, 18 px cards.
- Orange is for actions and emphasis, not large backgrounds.
- Green is reserved for availability/status dots.

## Required routes

- `snelleprijs.html` — primary path.
- `configurator.html` — detailed configuration.
- `offerte-vergelijker.html` — upload and compare 1–3 quotes.

## Image policy

- No AI people.
- Current architectural images are temporary visual placeholders and must be replaced with real company projects when available.
- Never use manufacturer production photography without a confirmed commercial licence.
- Do not show Eko-Okna as a brand.

## Brand wording

Use `RamenSpecialist.com` as the customer-facing brand. Show `by A.F. Arthub BV` in small, low-emphasis type. Do not claim that listed system brands are official partners unless this is documented; label the section “Systemen waarmee we werken”.
