## Goal

The homepage must guide homeowners aged 30–60 into one of three routes:

1. **Snelle Prijs** — realistic price indication in about two minutes.
2. **Configurator** — detailed configuration by position.
3. **OfferteCheck** — comparison of 1–3 offers by both price and technical content.

Primary CTA: **Bereken mijn prijs in 2 minuten**.
Secondary CTA: **Ik heb al een offerte — gratis vergelijken**.

## Fixed design decisions

- Light warm design with anthracite text and muted orange CTA.
- No AI-generated people.
- Temporary house visuals are allowed until real project photos are uploaded.
- Do not display Eko-Okna.
- Never claim to be the cheapest.
- Positioning: best balance between price, quality and complete execution.
- Brand endorsement: **RamenSpecialist.com** with **by A.F. Arthub BV** in small semi-transparent text.
- No fake ratings, project counts, partner claims or urgency.
- NL first; FR after the NL version is approved.

## Mobile and accessibility

- Body text minimum 16 px.
- Tap targets minimum 44 px.
- Single-column product/material layouts.
- Offerte comparison stacks or scrolls horizontally.
- Sticky primary CTA.
- Visible focus states.
- WCAG AA contrast target.
- Statuses must include text/icons, not colour only.
- No autoplaying music, sliders or immediate modal.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
